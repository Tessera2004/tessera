# Processing register – working document

| Activity | People | Data | Purpose | Retention | Recipients |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accounts and team access | Customer staff | Name, email, role, login metadata | SaaS access and security | Contract plus documented deletion period | Supabase |
| Customer and job management | Customer contacts and staff | Contact, address, job and note data | Contract performance | Customer-defined, subject to legal duties | Supabase |
| Time and evidence records | Customer staff | Work time, assignment, photo, signature | Work evidence and billing | Customer-defined and employment/accounting duties | Supabase |
| Billing | Customer billing contacts | Company and subscription metadata | Payment and accounting | Statutory accounting retention | Stripe, Supabase |
| Connected mailbox | Senders, recipients and customer staff | Email content and metadata | Customer communication | Customer configuration | Google, Microsoft or selected provider |
| Contact requests | Prospects | Contact details and message | Responding to request | Review and delete after 24 months at latest | Supabase |

The final register must name the actual owner, safeguards, project regions, deletion jobs and contractual documents before production launch.
