# Data incident response

1. Contain the incident: revoke sessions and secrets, disable affected functions and preserve evidence.
2. Identify affected tenants, data categories, people, period and likely consequences.
3. Inform affected customer contacts without delay under the DPA.
4. Assess whether the incident is likely to create a high risk and requires notification to the EDÖB and affected people.
5. Record decisions, times, evidence and remediation in the incident file.
6. Restore from a verified clean state and monitor for recurrence.
7. Complete a post-incident review and update controls, tests and documentation.
